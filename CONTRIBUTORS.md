## Important: this repository's starting history

The code in this repository was generated in a single working session, so any history that came with it does
not reflect who on your team did what. **That is not evidence of your individual contributions, and the teamwork
criterion (20% of the grade) is about exactly that.** Start your own history: each member should commit the area
they own, and make changes through their own pull requests, under their own name. Fill in the table above from
that real history, not from this file's template.
